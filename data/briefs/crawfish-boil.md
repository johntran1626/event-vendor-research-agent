# Brief: Bayline Pipe & Valve crawfish boil

Brief id: `crawfish-boil`. Machine-readable copy: `crawfish-boil.json` (same facts).

> Bayline Pipe & Valve throws a crawfish boil for its crews and their families every spring: 260 people outdoors, plenty of parking, a buffet, beer, and tents in case it rains. Same as the gala: three of each, the ones to skip, and the three that fit the budget together.
>
> Marisol Vance, Cattail & Rye Events

| | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Saturday, April 24, 2027, 12 to 5 PM (`2027-04-24`) |
| **Guests** | 260 |
| **Where** | anywhere within 30 miles of downtown Houston (the zip table in `docs/RUBRIC.md`) |
| **Venue** | Must have: outdoor space, on-site parking lot, allows outside caterers. Event rental fee $6,500 or less |
| **Caterer** | Must offer: buffet, on-site servers, bar service. $40 per person or less. Dietary: about 25 vegetarian guests and 2 kids with severe nut allergies, so the caterer must offer vegetarian, nut-free meals prepared separately |
| **Rentals** | Must rent: tents, tables, chairs. Full setup $28 per guest or less |
| **Total budget** | $23,000 for the venue, the caterer and the rentals together ("One budget for the whole event" in `docs/RUBRIC.md`) |

Every vendor must be open, in the Houston metro, free on the date, and big enough for 260 guests. The scoring rules are in `docs/RUBRIC.md`.
