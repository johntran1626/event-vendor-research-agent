# Brief: Tallgrass Literacy Fund spring gala

Brief id: `spring-gala`. Machine-readable copy: `spring-gala.json` (same facts).

> The Tallgrass Literacy Fund holds its spring gala on the first Saturday in March: 180 guests, a plated dinner, a bar, an auction on the big screen and a band after. They have one budget for the whole night, so the venue, the caterer and the rentals have to fit it together, not one at a time.
>
> Marisol Vance, Cattail & Rye Events

| | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Saturday, March 6, 2027, 6 to 11 PM (`2027-03-06`) |
| **Guests** | 180 |
| **Where** | anywhere within 15 miles of downtown Houston (the zip table in `docs/RUBRIC.md`) |
| **Venue** | Must have: indoor space, in-house AV, dance floor, allows outside caterers. Event rental fee $9,500 or less |
| **Caterer** | Must offer: plated dinner, on-site servers, bar service. $85 per person or less. Dietary: about 20 vegetarian guests and 9 who eat gluten-free, so the caterer must offer vegetarian, gluten-free |
| **Rentals** | Must rent: tables, linens, lighting. Full setup $22 per guest or less |
| **Total budget** | $26,800 for the venue, the caterer and the rentals together ("One budget for the whole event" in `docs/RUBRIC.md`) |

Every vendor must be open, in the Houston metro, free on the date, and big enough for 180 guests. The scoring rules are in `docs/RUBRIC.md`.
